## Official & Planning

Essential government entry portals, visa requirements, transit visa exemptions, and climate updates.

- [China National Tourist Office (Visit China)](http://www.cnto.org.cn/) - Official tourism board detailing cultural heritage sites, regional destinations, and visitor information.
- [China Visa Application Service Center](https://www.visaforchina.cn/) - Official service portal for processing entry visas, appointments, and consular requirements worldwide.
- [National Immigration Administration (NIA)](https://en.nia.gov.cn/) - Official government information on visa-free entry policies, 144-hour transit visas, and port-of-entry regulations.
- [China Meteorological Administration (CMA)](http://www.cma.gov.cn/en2014/) - National meteorological service monitoring forecasts, weather warnings, and seasonal climate conditions.

## Digital Payments & Super Apps

Key digital payment platforms essential for everyday transactions, transit, and services across China.

- [Alipay Tour Pass / International](https://www.alipay.com/) - Essential mobile payment platform enabling international bank card integration for foreign visitors.
- [WeChat Pay (International Cards)](https://pay.weixin.qq.com/) - Integrated messaging and payment app supporting international credit cards for everyday mobile payments.

## Transportation

Navigating high-speed rail networks, domestic aviation, and city metro systems.

- [12306 China Railway Official](https://www.12306.cn/en/) - Official national rail booking platform for high-speed (G/D trains) and conventional train tickets.
- [Trip.com Railway & Flights](https://www.trip.com/) - Primary English booking engine for Chinese domestic train tickets, flights, and hotel bookings.
- [Air China](https://www.airchina.com.cn/en/) - National flag carrier connecting major international gateways and domestic destinations.
- [China Southern Airlines](https://www.csair.com/en/) - Major domestic and international carrier centered around Asian and southern transit hubs.
- [China Eastern Airlines](https://www.ceair.com/) - Key airline servicing domestic, regional, and international air routes across East China.

## Destinations & Regions

### Beijing & North

- [Visit Beijing](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/) - Official municipal travel portal detailing the Forbidden City, Great Wall sections, and cultural landmarks.
- [Beijing Subway Official](https://www.bjsubway.com/) - Official transit map, fare calculator, and route planner for the Beijing subway network.

### Shanghai & East Coast

- [Meet in Shanghai](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/) - Official city tourism portal for exploring The Bund, French Concession, and modern districts.
- [Shanghai Metro](https://www.shmetro.com/) - Official guide for navigating Shanghai's extensive urban metro network.

### Sichuan & Southwest

- [Sichuan Tourism Bureau](http://www.sc.gov.cn/) - Official regional portal for Chengdu, Giant Panda breeding bases, and Jiuzhaigou National Park.
- [Yunnan Travel Guide](http://www.yn.gov.cn/) - Visitor resources for exploring Kunming, Dali, Lijiang Old Town, and Shangri-La.

### Guangdong & Greater Bay Area

- [Guangzhou Travel](http://www.guangzhou.gov.cn/) - Official tourism guide for Canton cultural sites, modern urban architecture, and transit links to Hong Kong/Macau.

### Silk Road & Northwest

- [Dunhuang & Mogao Grottoes Official](https://www.dhmg.org/) - Visitor center portal for ticket reservations and heritage guides to UNESCO Silk Road sites.

## Navigation & Connectivity

Mapping platforms, SIM services, and digital connectivity tools tailored for travelers in China.

- [Baidu Maps (Baidu Ditu)](https://map.baidu.com/) - Comprehensive mapping app providing real-time navigation and public transit routes across Chinese cities.
- [Amap (Gaode Ditu)](https://www.amap.com/) - Widely used navigation and ride-hailing integrated mapping system.
- [Nihao Mobile](https://www.nihaomobile.com/) - English-language SIM card and mobile service provider designed for international travelers and expats.

## Safety & Cultural Etiquette

Practical advice on emergency contacts, registration requirements, and local customs.

- [Foreigner Temporary Residence Registration](https://en.nia.gov.cn/) - Official overview regarding police registration rules required when staying outside commercial hotels.
- [110 China Police Assistance](http://www.mps.gov.cn/) - National emergency contact number for immediate police assistance across mainland China.
